
OLIST E-COMMERCE DATA ANALYSIS
Business Insights Report

1. Monthly Order Trend
November 2017 recorded 7,544 orders, the highest monthly order count
in the analysis. The business should review seasonal demand patterns
and plan inventory and customer support capacity ahead of peak periods.

2. Top Product Categories
Beauty & Health generated R$1,258,681.34 in item revenue, followed
by Watches & Gifts at R$1,205,005.68. The business should evaluate
stock availability and targeted promotions for these strong categories.

3. Order Fulfilment
Of 99,441 orders, 96,478 were marked delivered. The business should
investigate cancelled, unavailable and delayed orders to identify
opportunities to improve fulfilment.

4. Customer Reviews
The most common review score was 5, with 57,328 reviews, while
11,424 reviews received a score of 1. The business should study
low-scoring reviews to identify recurring customer problems.

5. Regional Sales
Regional sales differ across customer states. The business should
use the regional sales chart to identify high-performing markets
and investigate lower-performing regions before allocating promotions.

Most Surprising Finding
The dataset recorded 99,441 orders, with 96,478 marked as delivered.
Despite the high delivery count, the remaining order statuses show
why monitoring cancellations, unavailable orders and fulfilment
performance is important.
